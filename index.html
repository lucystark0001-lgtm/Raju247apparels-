Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions. Message us on WhatsApp for anything else.

How do I place an order?

Message us on WhatsApp with the style you like, your size and colour. We share real photos, confirm availability and the final price, then share payment and delivery details.

Do you take bulk and custom orders?

Yes. We handle bulk orders for retailers and events, and custom uniforms with logo printing or embroidery. Quantities, designs and pricing are confirmed over chat first.

How do I choose the right size?

Share your usual size or measurements on WhatsApp and we'll recommend the best fit using the size chart for that style.

What are the delivery, exchange and return terms?

Delivery charges, timelines and exchange or return terms depend on the product and order. These are confirmed with our team before you order.
